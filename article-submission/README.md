## Title
Soft Sensing of Melt Index in Multi-Grade Polyethylene Production: A Negative Result against the Last-Laboratory-Value Baseline

## Abstract (draft)
Soft sensors that estimate the melt index of polyethylene online are proposed to replace delayed laboratory results, but they must beat the simplest possible baseline. We evaluate soft sensors on the public industrial SRMG multi-grade polyethylene data (331 samples; research use only, after Liu, Gao and Chen, 2013). Linear models failed the validity checks, tree-based models reached an R² of about 0.3, and none of the models beat the baseline that repeats the last laboratory value of the same grade, which had an RMSE of 0.262. We report this as a negative result: with this small data set a soft sensor is not better than the information already available from the laboratory. Rolling-origin evaluation, just-in-time learning, a comparison with published SRMG results and the clarification of the adjacent filing 3869 are [to be measured]. The study gives plant engineers a clear benchmark that any proposed soft sensor for grade transitions should be required to beat.

**Keywords:** soft sensor; melt index; polyethylene; grade transition; negative result
